Demos

Things easier to show than to describe. Everything here runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

  • paladin — see what your agent reads integrity monitoring
    Paste a CLAUDE.md, .cursorrules or any instruction file and watch the characters your font refuses to draw appear: zero-width spaces, bidi overrides that reorder text on screen, and Cyrillic letters wearing Latin faces. Same scanner as the tool, generated from its source so the two cannot drift.
  • warden — what a decline is allowed to say rules engines
    A rules engine answers in its own vocabulary — rule ids, working-memory facts, the margin floor that priced the decision. Switch between a public, partner and internal caller and watch what crosses the boundary change, with everything held back listed underneath. Pinned against the Go binary's output for all three tiers.
