Demos

Things easier to show than to describe. Each one is the real Go tool compiled to WebAssembly and running in your browser — not a reimplementation of it. Nothing is uploaded.

  • paladin — see what your agent reads integrity monitoring
    Paste a CLAUDE.md, .cursorrules or any instruction file and watch the characters your font refuses to draw appear: zero-width spaces, bidi overrides that reorder text on screen, and Cyrillic letters wearing Latin faces. It runs paladin itself, compiled to WebAssembly — not a reimplementation.
  • warden — what a decline is allowed to say rules engines
    A rules engine answers in its own vocabulary — rule ids, working-memory facts, the margin floor that priced the decision. Switch between a public, partner and internal caller and watch what crosses the boundary change, with everything held back listed underneath. It runs warden itself, compiled to WebAssembly.
