What a decline is allowed to say

A rules engine answers in its own vocabulary: rule ids, working-memory facts, the margin floor that priced the decision. Hand that to whoever asked and consumers couple to names you can never rename — and an attacker who can submit transactions and read why they failed is running an oracle attack, binary-searching your thresholds one decline at a time. Tell them VELOCITY_LIMIT and they learn where the edge is. Tell them not_eligible and they learn nothing.
